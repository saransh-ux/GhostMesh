Communication survives. Even when the internet doesn't.

GhostMesh creates secure, encrypted mesh networks between nearby devices, keeping people connected during disasters, blackouts, and remote expeditions.

How it Works

Seamless connection, zero configuration.

radar

Step 1: Discover

Devices automatically locate nearby GhostMesh nodes via Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct.

route

Step 2: Hop

Messages bounce securely from device to device, extending range infinitely as long as nodes are present.

mark_email_read

Step 3: Deliver

Encrypted packets are reassembled at the destination, ensuring only the intended recipient can read them.
